## 1. State Agrarian Register — the process gate

**Source:** https://www.dar.gov.ua/ (fetched 3 August 2026)

Verified from the site's own wording:

- Open to all agricultural producers "незалежно від розміру господарства чи
  напряму діяльності" — regardless of holding size or activity.
- Accepts private individuals (including household plots), individual
  entrepreneurs (ФОП), and legal entities of any ownership form.
- Registration requires: a **qualified electronic signature (КЕП)**, an email
  address, a phone number, and a computer with an internet connection.
- Once registered, land, property and livestock data is pulled automatically
  from state cadastres and registries using the tax number.
- Registered users can apply to support programmes remotely and track status.

**Why the КЕП is called out as the obstacle:** it is the one requirement a
farmer is unlikely to already hold and cannot obtain online in the same sitting.
Everything else on that list they have.

## 2. Partial Credit Guarantee Fund in Agriculture

**Source:** https://pcgf.com.ua/ (fetched 3 August 2026)

Verified:

- Describes itself as a financial institution created to open access to
  financing for small and medium agricultural producers.
- Covers part of the lender's credit risk so banks can approve loans where the
  farmer lacks collateral.
- Supports micro, small and medium enterprises engaged in agriculture.
- **Applications go through a partner bank or non-bank financial institution,
  with a business plan. Not directly to the Fund.**

**Explicitly not stated on the homepage, and therefore not encoded:** the
guarantee percentage, any maximum limit, farm size or land area thresholds,
required legal form, and the list of partner institutions. Section 8 says so
rather than filling the gap.

## 3. Humanitarian demining compensation

**Source:** https://www.kmu.gov.ua/news/kompensatsii-na-3-miliardy-hryven-fermery-otrymaiut-vid-derzhavy-hroshi-na-humanitarne-rozminuvannia
(fetched 3 August 2026)

Verified, **as approved in 2024**:

- Administered by the Ministry of Economy; approved by the Cabinet of Ministers
  on 12 March 2024.
- "Держава компенсуватиме 80% вартості розмінування ділянки" — the state
  compensates 80% of the cost of demining a plot.
- Compensation is available once per plot.
- "Ціна на послуги з розмінування визначатиметься на відкритих торгах в системі
  Prozorro" — prices are set by open tender through Prozorro.
- UAH 3 billion allocated for 2024.
- Eligible: farmers working land they own or formally rent.
- Excluded: persons under Ukrainian sanctions; entities whose ultimate owners
  are citizens of the aggressor state; businesses in liquidation or bankruptcy;
  those with outstanding tax debt.

**Not stated in the source, and therefore not encoded:** the application route.
Section 9 says the route was not specified rather than inventing one.

**Vintage warning:** every figure above is 2024. Section 9 tags them as such and
instructs Kolos to confirm the current year before repeating any of them.

## Why the disqualifier list was generalised

The four exclusions above are verified for the demining programme only. Section
`PROCESS_REFERENCE` presents them as "verified for the demining compensation
programme and worth checking for any programme", not as a universal rule,
because only one instance was confirmed.

Tax debt is singled out because it is the one that catches farmers who are doing
nothing wrong, and it is fixable in advance if someone knows to look.
