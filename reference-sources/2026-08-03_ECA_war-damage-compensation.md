## What it establishes

**Programme:** partial compensation for business property destroyed or damaged
by Russian armed aggression, and partial compensation of insurance premiums
under war-risk insurance contracts. Two components, one programme.

**Legal basis:** the Порядок approved by Cabinet of Ministers Resolution
No. 1541 of 28 November 2025.

**Administered by:** ПрАТ «Експортно-кредитне агентство» (ECA).

**Property eligible under the damage component** — only that defined in
sub-paragraphs 2, 2-1 and 2-2 of paragraph 3 of the Порядок:

- виробниче обладнання — production equipment
- будівлі та споруди — buildings and structures
- окремі приміщення — individual premises
- інженерні мережі (комунікації) — engineering networks and utilities

Must belong to the business by right of ownership and meet the Порядок's other
requirements.

**Insurance premium component:** the Порядок contains no separate list of
insurable property types. Per paragraph 2 and sub-paragraph 5 of paragraph 3 it
applies to insured property owned by the business meeting the Порядок's other
requirements, "зокрема щодо місця розташування майна та умов договору
страхування" — notably the location of the property and the terms of the
insurance contract.

**Where to apply:** the ECA site carries the FAQ, application templates, and the
bank details for paying the required contribution (реквізити для сплати внеску).

**Limitation stated by the agency:** ECA has no power to form state policy or to
amend the Порядок. Those sit with the Cabinet of Ministers.

---

## What it does NOT establish

The reply states no amounts, no percentages, no deadlines and no application
window. Nothing of that kind may be attributed to this source. Section 7 of the
reference says so explicitly.

## Why this matters to Kolos

It fills a real gap. The **Rebuilding after war damage** front-page chip
previously had nothing better than the general 0.1% restoration-loan line from
the 5-7-9% programme to answer with. This is a dedicated compensation scheme
with a named administering body and a working application page.

It is also better sourced than anything else in the reference: sections 1 to 6
were compiled from web search, this came from the agency in writing.
